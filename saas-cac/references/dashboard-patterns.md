# Dashboard presentation patterns

Choose the smallest view that answers the decision. The CLI supports each pattern with `--view` and combines them with `--view full`.

1. **Executive KPI scorecard (`summary`)** — CAC, ARPA, monthly gross profit, payback, LTV, and LTV/CAC for a budget or operating review. Pair every KPI with its period and warnings.
2. **Channel efficiency table (`channels`)** — spend, acquired customers, CAC, and share by channel. Use for reallocation; call out unattributed or unreconciled totals.
3. **Funnel conversion table (`funnel`)** — stage volume, step conversion, cumulative conversion, and cost per stage outcome. Use to locate acquisition bottlenecks without confusing a lead with a paying customer.
4. **Period trend table (`trend`)** — CAC, payback, and their changes across comparable periods. Keep cost scope, customer definition, and revenue timing consistent.
5. **Cohort payback table (`cohorts`)** — cohort CAC, monthly gross profit per acquired customer, and estimated payback. Use when cohorts differ materially in price, retention, or channel mix.

For a new audience, start with the scorecard and one decision-specific view. Use all five only when the data exists and the additional detail changes a decision.
